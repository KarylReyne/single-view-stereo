\subsection{Attention Weights of Guidance Prompts}\label{attn-weights}
\reffig{fig:attn-maps} shows the aggregated attention scores of a conditioning prompt for a sample from our dataset. The cross-attention module seems to be able to faithfully seperate the concepts for e.g. the tokens \emph{car} and \emph{museum} in the image, while both uni- and bi-directional attention seem to not attend to a particular object in the image for the same tokens. This indicates that uni-/bi-directional attention may be unsuitable for conditioning with null-text inversion, although our evaluation currently does not support this assumption as the mean generation quality of \mono{eval3} is not significantly higher than that of \mono{eval4} (see \reffig{fig:quality-mean}).
